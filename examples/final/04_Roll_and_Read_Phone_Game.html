R-Controlled Vowels and Tricky Words

Grab a real die if you like. Four rows. Twenty-four words.

Words and directions are from the teacher's sheet. Game parts are NEW and not educator-reviewed.

How to play

    Ways to Play

      NEWIn this game
      • The die rolls on screen. Using your own die? Tap its number instead.
      • Tap Cover it to hide the word, then Uncover to check.
      • You tap whether you read it right. That is your tally mark.
      • Partner race has its own button on the start screen.
      • Write it works great with a notebook beside you.
      • Sort it is not in the game. Sorting needs a teacher's check.

      About this game

      From the teacher's sheet

      The title, all 24 words, the directions, the Ways to Play, and the three tricky words (Very, Every, Before). They are shown word for word.

      NEWAdded for the game

      The rolling die, sounds, stars, the trail, the word album, the Hear it button, the partner scoreboard, and every encouraging message. None of it is from the original sheet and none of it is educator-reviewed.

      No answer key is supplied. The player (or a grown-up) taps whether a word was read right.

      Privacy

      No names, no accounts, no tracking, no internet. The word album is saved only on this device, in this browser, and you can clear it.

      For grown-ups

      Ten to fifteen minutes can be plenty. Stop sooner if they are tired. A few words, read happily, is a good day. Let your child try first and praise effort ("You sounded that out!"). If a word is stuck, say it together and move on.

      Not a substitute for reading instruction. Permission to share the original sheet is not confirmed.

      Partner race

      • Partner race: Both players roll, read their word, and the first correct reader earns a point.
      NEWHow it works here
      • Four rounds, one for each row, A to D.
      • Each player taps their own die (or their own number if using a real die).
      • Both words flip up. Read them out loud.
      • A grown-up or the players tap who read first and got it right. Nobody right? Tap Neither.
      • After each round: cover it, spell it, uncover it to check.
