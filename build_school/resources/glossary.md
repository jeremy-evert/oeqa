# Glossary

- **Command:** a typed instruction to the computer.
- **Terminal / PowerShell:** a window where you type commands.
- **Prompt:** the message you give an AI.
- **Demonstrated:** you saw it happen yourself.
- **Claimed:** someone or something said it, and you have not checked.
- **Unverified:** nobody has checked.
- **Read-only:** looks but does not change anything.
- **Directory / folder:** a place that holds files.
- **Evidence:** something you can look at that shows whether a claim is true.
