# Prompt cards

Copy one. Paste it into any AI assistant. Change the part in [brackets].

**Explain before running**
> I'm learning how to use my computer. Explain the [Windows PowerShell / Mac Terminal] command `[command]` without running it. Tell me what it does, whether it changes anything, why it could be useful, and how I could check that your explanation is correct. Keep it simple.

**What could go wrong?**
> Before I try `[command]`, what could it read, what could it change, and how would I undo or stop it? Say clearly if you are not sure.

**Check yourself**
> Which parts of your last answer are things you are certain of, which are things you are guessing, and what could I do to check each one?

**Say it simpler**
> Explain that again for someone who has never used a command line. One short paragraph, no jargon.
