# Start here

You do not need to install anything. You do not need to know how to code.

## Your first five minutes

1. Open **Lesson 00**: `lessons/00_start/index.html` (a web page) or `lessons/00_start/LESSON.md` (text).
2. Copy the one prompt into any AI assistant.
3. Read the answer. Use the three checks in the lesson to decide whether to trust it.

That is the whole first step. If you stop there, you have learned the most important habit in this school:

> **Ask the computer to explain itself before you follow its instructions.**

## What comes next

Only if you want to. `CURRICULUM.md` shows the road: 21 small steps in five chapters. Most are not written yet. You will see which ones are real in the status column.

That is useful. Write one sentence about what confused you in `sidecar/LESSON_INBOX.md`. The next learner gets an easier road because you did.
